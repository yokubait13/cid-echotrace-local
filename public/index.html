CID EchoTrace Local

A clear path from evidence to transcript.

  1. Your accuracy-focused local model is already included. Whisper Large v3 Turbo multilingual is installed with CID EchoTrace—there is nothing to configure or download.
  2. Drop a recording. The app streams it only to its local service at 127.0.0.1.
  3. Review, label, and export. Stereo recordings receive local Speaker A/B channel labels; click a tag to rename it, or assign tags to mono segments before downloading TXT, SRT, and a branded PDF transcript.

Whisper, the ICMV decoder bridge, FFmpeg's broad local decoder suite, local speech detection, and the transcription model are packaged inside CID EchoTrace. The installed edition also includes its CUDA runtime; an NVIDIA display driver is all GPU acceleration requires.
